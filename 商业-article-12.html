Agency as the Heart: A Philosophical Shift in AI Tool Design

4/12/2026, 3:31:11 PM

📌 One-Sentence Summary

Frank Wang challenges the 'agency as a muscle' metaphor， arguing that human agency is actually the 'heart'—the innate ability to care and direct—which AI cannot replicate.

📝 Summary

In response to Notion founder Ivan Zhao's metaphor that 'agency is a muscle，' Frank Wang (lifesinger) proposes a counter-perspective: agency is the 'heart.' He argues that while AI can outperform human 'muscles' in execution (writing， designing， analyzing)， it cannot replicate the human ability to care and provide direction. This philosophical distinction leads to two different approaches in tool building: one focused on making users 'stronger' (muscle) and the other on asking what the user 'wants to create' (heart). He notes that his new project， YouMind， is being built around the latter philosophy.
