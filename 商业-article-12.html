Scaling Issues in AI vs Biology: Open Questions

10/16/2026, 6:07:24 PM

📌 一句话摘要

The author outlines three core problems in AI-driven biology: scaling， target biology constraints， and competitive moats， emphasizing the need for a closed-loop model-to-experiment cycle.

📝 详细摘要

The tweet examines three key challenges in applying AI to biology， highlighting the difficulty of scaling due to highly context-dependent data， the limited impact of AI progress in drug discovery because of target biology， toxicity， and efficacy constraints， and the shift of competitive moats toward proprietary data， negative data， wet lab automation， and clinical feedback. It calls for a closed-loop cycle of model， hypothesis， experiment， ground truth， and model update to truly advance AI in biology.

💡 主要观点

  1. Scaling challenge due to context-dependent biology Biological data is highly context-sensitive and expensive to generate， making scalable AI difficult. Success of AlphaFold does not imply the whole biology field can be AlphaFolded.
  2. AI advances fall short in critical drug development issues While molecule generation and structure prediction improve， real drugs often fail because of target biology nuances， toxicity， and human efficacy limits.
  3. Competitive moat shift toward data and automation As AI models become commodity， scarce assets transform into proprietary data， negative results， wet lab automation， and clinical feedback， defining new competitive barriers.
  4. Need for a closed-loop experimental cycle True progress requires iterating through Model → Hypothesis → Experiment → Ground Truth → Model Update to validate and improve AI predictions.
