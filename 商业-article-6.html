Google TPU v7 Benchmark Outperforms NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 in Cost-Performance Ratio

10/15/2026, 2:43:16 AM

📌 一句话摘要

The tweet discusses new benchmark results where Google TPU v7 running FP8 vLLM demonstrated a 1.67x better performance-per-dollar ratio than NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 running FP4.

📝 详细摘要

Google has submitted its first TPU v7 FP8 vLLM results to the Agentic Inference Benchmark， showing a 1.67x better performance per dollar compared to NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 FP4. The author analyzes whether this externalized TPU offering represents a significant risk to NVIDIA's AI market monopoly.

💡 主要观点

  1. TPU v7 shows distinct cost-performance advantage Google's TPU v7 running FP8 precision demonstrated 1.67x better performance per dollar compared to NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72 running FP4 in the Agentic Inference Benchmark.
  2. Strategic challenge to NVIDIA's compute monopoly Google is actively pushing to make its TPU power accessible externally， offering an increasingly competitive and cost-effective alternative to NVIDIA's standard architectures.

💬 文章金句

  • Google 首次向 Agentic Inference Benchmark（智能体推理基准测试）提交了采用 FP8 精度、基于 vLLM 的 TPU v7 测试结果。测试显示，与 NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 使用 FP4 精度运行相比，Google 的方案甚至实现了 高达 1.67 倍的每美元性能优势。
