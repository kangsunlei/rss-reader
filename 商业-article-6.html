Places Close to Communism: Free Knowledge Platforms in China

11/20/2026, 3:30:00 AM

📌 一句话摘要

Lists various digital platforms in China that provide free access to knowledge， media， and tools， positioning them as examples of near-communist ideals due to their open and free nature.

📝 详细摘要

The tweet enumerates several platforms available in China that offer free access to knowledge， entertainment， and productivity tools. It includes Z-Library， AO3， Sci-Hub， Wikipedia， SoundCloud， Linux， Figma， Substack， Bilibili， GitHub， Telegram， ilovepdf， BitTorrent， F-Droid， Pocket Casts， and a Chinese translation of classic texts. The author suggests these represent near-communist ideals because they provide free， open access to resources typically restricted or monetized elsewhere.

💡 主要观点

  1. Free access to knowledge as a value Platforms like Z-Library， Sci-Hub， and Wikipedia embody the principle of freely sharing information， reducing barriers to learning and creativity.
  2. Decentralized and community-driven models Many listed platforms (e.g.， BitTorrent， Telegram) operate without centralized control， enabling user-driven content distribution and collaboration.

💬 文章金句

  • The place closest to communism in this world is probably these:
  • Z-Library (Zlib) — millions of free e-books， textbooks， and journals， regardless of copyright status.
  • Sci-Hub — bypasses paywalls to make all academic papers freely available.
  • GitHub — programmers can freely store and collaborate on code， view and fork any public project.
