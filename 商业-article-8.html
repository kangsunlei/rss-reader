This tweet highlights a creative AI workflow for video production. The author praises the process as 'real guts' and 'awesome'， noting that the creator Eian documented their complete pipeline: collaborating with Claude to refine story and storyboard， using Codex to generate character and scene visuals along with keyframe art， applying Seedance for video generation， and finally editing everything together with code.

💡 主要观点

  1. Claude is used to discuss story and storyboard details first This step ensures the narrative structure and visual sequence are well-defined before moving to visual generation.
  2. Codex generates character designs， scenes， and keyframe illustrations Codex provides the visual assets needed for each storyboard moment， automating the artistic component.
  3. Seedance produces the final video from generated visuals Seedance handles the video synthesis， converting illustrated keyframes into moving video content.
  4. Code is used to edit and compile the final video This demonstrates a hybrid approach combining AI image/video generation with traditional post-production scripting for precision control.
