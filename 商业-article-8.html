Y2A-Auto: Automated YouTube to Bilibili/AcFun Content Pipeline

11/9/2026, 2:37:00 AM

📌 一句话摘要

A GitHub-discovered open-source tool that automatically downloads YouTube videos， translates them， generates subtitles， and publishes to Bilibili and AcFun with built-in content moderation.

📝 详细摘要

The tweet introduces Y2A-Auto， an open-source automated pipeline that downloads YouTube videos， processes them with AI translation and auto-generated subtitles for foreign-language content， and automatically uploads to Bilibili and AcFun. It features real-time monitoring of new uploads， content moderation to avoid sensitive topics， and set-and-forget operation. The tool is positioned as a way to capitalize on information asymmetries in vertical domains.

💡 主要观点

  1. Fully automated content pipeline Downloads YouTube videos and uploads to Bilibili/AcFun automatically without manual intervention
  2. Built-in AI translation and subtitle generation Converts foreign-language content into Chinese with automatic subtitles， enabling cross-border content repurposing
  3. Content moderation to avoid sensitive topics Prevents account bans by filtering out prohibited content during the upload process
