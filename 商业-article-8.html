Open-source Tool rea-video Decomposes Viral Videos Frame-by-Frame to Reveal Creation Methods

11/8/2026, 1:35:00 AM

📌 一句话摘要

An open-source AI tool called rea-video analyzes viral videos to determine whether they are real footage， AI-generated， or code-rendered， break down cinematography elements， trace AI prompts， and generate a complete replication plan.

📝 详细摘要

rea-video is an open-source AI tool with over 3700 stars that can analyze videos to detect whether they are real footage， AI-generated， or computer-rendered. It examines camera movements， lighting， transitions， subtitles， and background music. It can reverse-engineer AI prompts used in specific shots and provide a full replication strategy indicating which parts should be shot physically， handled by AI， or created via code. It even identifies timing details such as subtitle appearances and sound effects. This tool is valuable for studying viral content on platforms like AI advertisements and Douyin/TikTok.

💡 主要观点

  1. Determines video origin (real vs AI vs code-rendered) Uses computer vision to classify whether video content was captured in real life， generated by AI models， or rendered through programming techniques.
  2. Analyzes cinematography elements Breaks down camera movements (运镜)， lighting (打光)， scene transitions (转场)， subtitles， and background music to understand video production techniques.
  3. Reverse-engineers AI prompts Traces back the likely AI prompts used for generating specific video shots， enabling replication of effects.
  4. Generates complete replication blueprint Provides a structured plan showing which elements require live-action filming， which should be handled by AI generation， and which can be achieved through code/rendering.
  5. Extracts detailed timing information Identifies precise timestamps for camera movements， subtitle appearances， and sound effect triggers， useful for precise video replication.
