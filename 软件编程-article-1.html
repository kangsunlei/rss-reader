The Myth of the 'No-Code Advantage' in the AI Era

3/8/2026, 7:43:56 PM

📌 一句话摘要

Gergely Orosz challenges the notion that AI renders coding skills obsolete， drawing a parallel to how smartphones haven't replaced the need for professional filmmaking expertise.

📝 详细摘要

Gergely Orosz addresses the emerging narrative that AI enables non-developers to build software more effectively than engineers. Using a sharp analogy， he argues that just as the ubiquity of smartphones and editing apps didn't eliminate the need for professional filmmaking knowledge， AI tools do not negate the fundamental necessity of understanding software engineering. He encourages developers to ignore the hype and remain focused on continuous learning and hands-on building.
