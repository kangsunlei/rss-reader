📝 详细摘要

The 2.7.0 release of MiaoYan， a native macOS Markdown editor， is now live. Designed for engineers who prioritize a clean， AI-free writing environment， this update features official Apple notarization to resolve security warnings， significant stability improvements to the preview engine， and refined editing controls. Additionally， the release prepares for an App Store launch with sandbox entitlements and expanded i18n support， emphasizing long-term performance and maintainability.
