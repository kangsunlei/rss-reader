📝 详细摘要

Vanessa Lee (Shopify VP of Product， reporting directly to CEO) discusses Shopify's transition from traditional SaaS to agent-led commerce. She shares the 5-pillar framework for AI-native product management， how to use LLM-based judges to evaluate AI product quality， and the "courage as a service' leadership philosophy. The video also explores why technical product managers need to understand API details and her case study of successfully convincing the CEO to version Shopify's API.

💡 主要观点

  1. AI-Native Transformation Platform transition from traditional SaaS to agent-led commerce
  2. 5-Pillar Framework Design， evaluation， safety， architecture， and adoption product framework
  3. LLM Judge System Internal tools using AI to evaluate AI output quality
  4. Courage as a Service Technical leadership requires deep details and willingness to drive change
