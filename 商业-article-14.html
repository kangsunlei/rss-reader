AI Ghostwriting and the Death of Amazon's 6-Pager

10/19/2026, 7:25:01 PM

📌 一句话摘要

The author argues that AI-generated content is eroding the purpose of Amazon's famous '6-pager' writing culture， which was originally designed to force deep thinking.

📝 详细摘要

The tweet discusses how AI is undermining Amazon's corporate culture of '6-pagers' (narrative memos used instead of PPTs to ensure rigorous thinking). The author points out a paradox: while the form of the 6-pager remains， the intellectual effort is now outsourced to AI—prompts replace deep thought， and AI summaries replace careful reading—effectively killing the 'soul' of the process.

💡 主要观点

  1. Erosion of Deep Thinking The 6-pager was intended to force authors to think through problems rigorously; AI ghostwriting allows users to bypass this cognitive effort.
  2. The 'AI;DR' Cycle A cycle is formed where AI writes the long-form content and the reader consumes an AI-generated summary， rendering the original long-form requirement obsolete.

💬 文章金句

  • 讽刺的是，这个制度的本意是逼人"想清楚"；AI 代笔之后，"想清楚"这一步也被外包了。
