Concluding the thread on 'make something people want，' Paul Graham offers a reassuring perspective: once a startup achieves product-market fit by making something genuinely desired by users， the most significant hurdle is overcome. He suggests that while other mistakes might occur， they are largely 'edge cases' or secondary issues， implying that solving this core problem simplifies the entrepreneurial journey significantly. This emphasizes the paramount importance of user demand in a startup's success.
