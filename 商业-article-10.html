Open-source AI Comic Script Creation Toolkit

11/11/2026, 3:17:23 AM

📌 一句话摘要

Introduces an open-source toolkit that provides 21 comic creation templates for generating AI-powered animated stories across multiple genres.

📝 详细摘要

The post introduces 'AI Comic Skills'， an open-source project offering 21 different creation templates for various comic genres like mystery， fantasy， and urban reverse. The toolkit enables automated workflow from story concept to video generation prompts， capable of producing 30 episodes of 4-minute vertical comics. Users can leverage tools like Claude Code， WorkBuddy， or DeepSeek to execute the templates without additional dependencies.

💡 主要观点

  1. 21 genre-diverse comic creation templates Covers popular short drama genres including mystery， fantasy， urban reverse， and palace intrigue， addressing market demand for varied content types.
  2. Automated end-to-end workflow Streamlines process from story brainstorming to character setup， script writing， storyboard prompts， video generation prompts， and publication copy creation.
  3. Platform-flexible execution Templates work across multiple AI coding platforms including Claude Code， WorkBuddy， and DeepSeek， requiring no additional dependencies.
