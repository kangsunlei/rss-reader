The Rise of Taste， Human Authenticity and Judgment in an AI World | Adam Mosseri (Head of Instagram)

7/13/2026, 5:21:51 AM

📌 一句话摘要

Instagram Head Adam Mosseri discusses how AI is fundamentally reshaping product teams into smaller， generalist "squads，" and why human taste， judgment， and authenticity become the most crucial competitive advantages when creation becomes effortless.

📝 详细摘要

In a deep conversation with Lenny Rachitsky， Instagram Head Adam Mosseri dissects the tectonic shifts AI is causing in product development. He details Instagram's new "squad" model， shrinking cross-functional teams from ten specialists to four to six generalist engineers and a "Product Specialist"—a new role that absorbs design， data science， and research functions. Mosseri argues that as AI commoditizes execution， the value shifts upstream to deciding *what* to build， making taste and judgment the last bastions of human advantage. He explores how this changes hiring， emphasizing curiosity， resilience， and a willingness to be wrong over managing large organizations. The discussion also covers Instagram's algorithm evolution， the balance between algorithm-driven feeds and user control， and why the flood of AI-generated content is a tailwind for platforms centered on human creators， making authentic perspective more sought-after than ever.

💡 主要观点

  1. The "squad" model replaces complex cross-functional teams with leaner， generalist units. Instagram is moving to teams of four to six generalist engineers plus a 'Product Specialist'， who handles design， data science， and research. This reduces coordination overhead and speeds up decision-making， challenging the traditional role of specialists.
  2. In an AI-driven world， human taste and judgment become the ultimate differentiators. Since AI can now execute almost any idea， the critical skill is deciding *what* to build. Mosseri emphasizes that taste is hard to automate and will define success， especially for those transitioning from design backgrounds.
  3. The best product leaders act as curators， not just creative visionaries. Top leaders create an environment where great ideas can emerge from anywhere and excel at selecting， nurturing， and aligning the best concepts， rather than needing to originate every good idea themselves.
  4. AI-generated content makes human authenticity more valuable， which is a tailwind for Instagram. In a world flooded with synthetic content， people will more actively seek genuine human perspectives. Instagram， as the largest platform for individual creators， stands to benefit from this shift towards valuing real people behind the content.
  5. Maximizing AI tools requires a clear-eyed understanding of their specific strengths and current limitations. Success with AI comes not from blind faith or broad rejection， but from having a nuanced intuition about what a model is good at today and what it will likely be good at in the near future.

💬 文章金句

  • In a world where it gets easier and easier to just make stuff， it becomes more important to make sure your time is spent figuring out what the hell you should be doing in the first place.
  • If you just ask an LLM for a strategy， you're going to get something that's very predictable... If you want a strategy that's really going to work， it's going to take a long time to just think through carefully what are the different inputs that need to be considered.
  • In a world awash with synthetic content， I actually think people are going to go out of their way to go find creativity and authenticity and people， not less.
  • I think the people that are going to maximize the value they get out of AI are those that have a very clear understanding of what AI is good at and what it's not， but also have an intuition and a nose for what it's going to be good at and what it's not in the next month or two.
  • When you try to learn another language， the most important thing is， can you just get the words out of your mouth? ... With all these new tools and new models and new technologies， I feel like it's the same thing. You just gotta be willing to try.
