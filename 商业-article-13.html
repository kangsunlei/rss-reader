Hacker Exposes Personal Identity via AI Resume Task

10/8/2026, 11:56:00 PM

📌 一句话摘要

A CrowdStrike report reveals a Chinese hacker's identity after he used Claude Code to write a resume that included detailed logs of his cyberattacks.

📝 详细摘要

According to a CrowdStrike report， a 26-year-old man from Maoming， Guangdong， is suspected of attacking several South Korean banks. The suspect used ARTEX， a Chinese open-source AI penetration testing tool， alongside LLMs. His identity was compromised because he used Claude Code to generate a 'security researcher resume，' inadvertently including his hacker achievements， Telegram handle， age， education， and home address in the prompt and output logs.

💡 主要观点

  1. AI as a double-edged sword for offensive security The suspect used specialized AI tools like ARTEX for attacks， but was caught due to a lack of operational security when interacting with consumer AI like Claude.
  2. Tracing via LLM interaction logs CrowdStrike managed to deanonymize the hacker by analyzing resume-building requests that contained sensitive personal and tactical information.
