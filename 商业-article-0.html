Defining Entrance Behavior in Event Data Analysis

11/15/2026, 5:30:28 AM

📌 一句话摘要

This tweet explains a common SQL query strategy to analyze purchase behavior， which filters visitors by their initial interaction type (page_view) to calculate effective purchase counts.

📝 详细摘要

The analysis filters the event_data table to include only visitors whose first interaction was a page_view， establishing the visitor's entry point. From there， it counts the number of subsequent purchase events to determine effective purchase counts， with a minimum count of zero.

💡 主要观点

  1. Entry Point Definition The logic establishes an 'entrance' for each visitor based on their first recorded event， focusing only on those who arrived via a page_view.
  2. Effective Purchase Count By tracking events chronologically after the entry point， the system accurately counts completed purchases， distinguishing these from initial browsing sessions.

  • 给每个访客定一个“入口”，只统计从浏览页面进来的人。
