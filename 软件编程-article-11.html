📝 详细摘要

This tweet addresses the ongoing 'AI bubble' debate by providing empirical evidence on developer adoption. It compares two distinct datasets: research from DX showing significant usage (44% daily， 75% weekly)， and a survey from The Pragmatic Engineer—which skews toward Big Tech and startups—showing near-ubiquitous adoption (83% daily， 95% weekly). The data indicates that AI tools have already become a fundamental component of the professional development workflow， countering the argument that the technology is driven solely by speculative hype.
