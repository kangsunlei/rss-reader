The author reviews 'Readout'， a native macOS tool designed to visualize the Claude Code environment in real time. It features comprehensive tracking of agents， sessions， repositories， costs， and MCP (Model Context Protocol) ports. The app also supports instant global search and full session replays with timeline playback. Notably， it operates entirely locally without requiring an account， offering a highly polished experience even in its beta stage.
