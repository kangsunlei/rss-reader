Pipecat: Python Framework for Modular Real-Time Voice AI Applications

11/8/2026, 4:06:00 AM

📌 一句话摘要

Pipecat is a Python framework that modularizes STT， LLM， TTS， and transport layers for building real-time voice AI applications with low latency.

📝 详细摘要

Pipecat is a Python framework that modularizes speech-to-text (STT)， large language models (LLM)， text-to-speech (TTS)， and transport layer components into plug-and-play modules. It supports WebRTC and WebSocket for low-latency real-time conversations， handles streaming audio and video， and integrates with major providers like Deepgram， Whisper， OpenAI， Anthropic， Gemini， ElevenLabs， and Cartesia. It enables the creation of voice assistants， customer service agents， AI companions， and multi-agent collaborations.

💡 主要观点

  1. Modular architecture for voice AI components Provides plug-and-play modules for speech-to-text， language models， text-to-speech， and transport layers， enabling flexible composition based on specific needs.
  2. Low-latency real-time communication support Uses WebRTC and WebSocket protocols to maintain low latency in conversational AI applications.
  3. Broad integration ecosystem Supports major providers across STT (Deepgram， Whisper)， LLM (OpenAI， Anthropic， Gemini)， and TTS (ElevenLabs， Cartesia， OpenAI Realtime， Gemini Multimodal Live).
  4. Multiple AI application scenarios Suitable for voice assistants， customer service agents， AI companions， and multi-agent collaborative systems.
