Feasibility Calculation of Brute-Forcing Mnemonic Phrases

10/9/2026, 12:31:39 AM

📌 一句话摘要

A technical debunking of the possibility of brute-forcing 24-word seed phrases with multiple errors using mobile hardware.

📝 详细摘要

The author provides a mathematical analysis to debunk a claim about brute-forcing mnemonic phrases on a mobile phone. If three positions are unknown， the combinations exceed 8.5 billion. Even with checksum optimizations reducing candidates to 33.6 million， the PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 iterations required would take months or years on a single-threaded mobile CPU， making the original claim of a quick fix highly improbable.

💡 主要观点

  1. Mathematical impossibility of mobile brute-forcing for 3+ errors The entropy of a 24-word phrase with unknown errors is too high for the PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 derivation speed of a mobile device like a Pixel 6a.
